## 📝 Project Description (Copy & Paste)

```markdown
Architected and deployed Global FuelPulse AI, a full-stack, institutional-grade energy market decision intelligence and econometric forecasting platform. Live continuous coverage up to October 2026 across 150+ countries and key petroleum benchmarks.

🚀 Live Interactive Dashboard: https://global-fuelpulse-ai-jqsi5ngrigi4hm4gtmcghn.streamlit.app
💻 GitHub Repository: https://github.com/alihamza2912/global-fuelpulse-ai

Key Engineering & Analytics Highlights:
• Automated Multi-Source Ingestion: Built robust ETL connectors ingesting live weekly feeds from the U.S. EIA, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), India PPAC (MoPNG), European Commission Weekly Oil Bulletin, Open Exchange Rates (23 FX currencies), and UN Comtrade.
• Relational Data Architecture: Implemented a dual-engine architecture (MySQL primary with automated SQLite fallback) managing 13 normalized tables and over 1,400+ validated weekly price observations.
• Econometric & Risk Models: Formulated an interactive 7-stage Fuel Price Build-Up waterfall (Base, Refining, OMC Margin, Central Excise, State VAT), Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) for bilateral supplier concentration, and vulnerability monitoring across 6 global maritime chokepoints (Hormuz, Malacca, Suez).
• ML Time-Series Forecasting: Deployed Holt-Winters Exponential Smoothing and Auto-ARIMA models generating 4-week projections with 95% confidence intervals, rigorously validated against naive persistence baselines with true holdout MAE and RMSE metrics.
• Guarded AI Market Analyst: Integrated Google Gemini Free-Tier (Flash) with an atomic multi-user quota manager, daily midnight PT reset synchronization, question caching, and direct SQL routing to achieve zero-quota execution on simple queries.
• Editorial Industrial UX: Crafted an editorial design system with Plotly visualizations, light-mode BaseWeb portal styling, and WCAG AA contrast compliance.
```
